Episode 98. Sam Bankman-Fried.
Today: the rise and fall of FTX.

Sam Bankman-Fried founded the crypto exchange FTX in 2019, and within two years it had over a million users. In November 2022, reports exposed its tangled relationship with Alameda Research, his crypto trading firm, which acted as a market maker on FTX. Customers rushed to withdraw, rival exchange Binance dumped FTX's token, briefly offered to buy the company and then walked away, and FTX filed for bankruptcy.

Bankman-Fried was arrested in December 2022 on charges including wire fraud, securities fraud and money laundering. His closest associates, including former Alameda chief Caroline Ellison and FTX executives Gary Wang and Nishad Singh, cooperated with prosecutors, and his bail was revoked in 2023 over alleged witness tampering.

Since then: in November 2023 a jury convicted him on all counts, and in 2024 he was sentenced to twenty-five years in prison.
