Episode 3. Currency Strength.
Currency Appreciation: (1) Strong currencies reduce import prices. (2) Strong currencies facilitate debt servicing, when debts are denominated in foreign currencies. (3) Domestic firms can merge with and, or acquire foreign firms more cheaply when the domestic currency appreciates. (4) Domestic firms' resource imports (for example, oil, technology) are cheaper, hence lowering inflation. Currency Depreciation: (1) Exports are more competitive with weak currencies. (2) Weak currencies increase tourism. (3) Currency depreciation reduces imports and increases exports, helping to balance trade deficits.
