Episode 95. Poland's Elections.
Today: Poland's October 2023 election, as the file saw it beforehand, and what happened next.

First, the stakes. Jarosław Kaczyński's right-wing Law and Justice party, known as PiS, sought an unprecedented third term, and the opposition, led by Donald Tusk's Civic Coalition, called it the most important election since the fall of communism.

Second, the issues. PiS strongly backed Ukraine but clashed with the EU over Ukrainian grain imports. It made migration the centerpiece, with a strongly anti-immigrant, anti-Muslim campaign in an overwhelmingly Catholic country. The opposition attacked it over record migration and a scandal in which consular officials sold visas for bribes. PiS campaigned on Catholic family values, raising child benefits sharply and boosting public-sector pay. And it was accused of democratic backsliding by packing the Constitutional Tribunal and Supreme Court, which led the EU to freeze tens of billions of euros in recovery funds.

Since then: PiS came first but lost its majority, and Tusk became prime minister at the head of a broad coalition in December 2023, unlocking EU funds. But in 2025 the PiS-backed candidate Karol Nawrocki won the presidency, setting up a standoff with Tusk's government.
